## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: An opened rhythm row draws vertical ratio's stride

In the wide layout, an opened rhythm row whose focus draws vertical ratio's stride (vertical ratio
measured, its stride measured) SHALL point the runner at the stride camera, at the rhythm area's full
speed, instead of the whole figure. An opened knees row SHALL play slowed with the "Slow motion"
note, as the tour's knees stop does.

#### Scenario: Opening Rhythm with a measured stride

- **WHEN** the reader, in the wide layout, opens Rhythm and vertical ratio's stride is measured
- **THEN** the camera is the stride camera, the figure shows the bounce and stride brackets, and no
  "Slow motion" note shows

#### Scenario: Opening Knees

- **WHEN** the reader, in the wide layout, opens Knees
- **THEN** the camera frames the legs and the figure plays slowed with a visible "Slow motion" note
