## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A side stop's still beat shows its headline's evidence footage

On a landing or knees stop, the tour SHALL pass the runner the stop's headline metric's evidence
frame as footage when that metric's evidence from its winning clip has arrived, the metric is one
of overstriding, foot strike, knee flexion or knee drive, the winning clip is classified a side
view, the clip's travel direction is known, the frame has a hip, and its measured side resolves.
The runner then shows it behind the still figure (model-runner, "A still figure can show its
measured instant's footage behind it, faintly"). Every other stop, a front or ambiguous winning
clip, and the intro SHALL pass none. The tour SHALL still mount no evidence canvas: the runner
copies the pixels.

#### Scenario: A landing stop over a side clip under reduced motion
- **WHEN** the reader reaches the landing stop under reduced motion, and the overstriding frame from its winning side clip has arrived
- **THEN** the runner's footage plate shows that frame behind the still figure

#### Scenario: A front clip, or a stop without a side camera
- **WHEN** the stop's winning clip is classified front or ambiguous, or the stop is rhythm, posture, arms, foot path or the one-thing stop
- **THEN** no footage plate shows

#### Scenario: The intro
- **WHEN** the intro is running
- **THEN** no footage plate shows
