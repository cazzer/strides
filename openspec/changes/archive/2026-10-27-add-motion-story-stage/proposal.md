## Why

The analysis currently yields measurements and evidence cards, but no continuous visual connection between a runner's intent, the processing interval, and the measured form in each result.

## What Changes

Add a single 2D motion stage beside the existing results. During analysis it shows a clearly labeled illustrative running skeleton. Once measurements are ready, it transitions once to detected pose data. As the reader scrolls or focuses a metric, the stage shows that metric's actual source clip evidence faintly under a crisp detected skeleton, when evidence is available.

## Scope

This is an explanatory visual, not an ideal-form score or a new biometric. Existing metric calculations, evidence acquisition, and clip sampling stay unchanged.
